## Plot
peso: 20
descrizione: Originality, coherence, pacing
- Does the premise stand out from similar stories I know?
- Did the beginning hook me within the first chapters?
- Do events arise from one another, without convenient coincidences?
- Is the plot free of logical holes or contradictions?
- Is there a clear central conflict with concrete stakes?
- Does the story move along without useless, repetitive or padded parts?
- Are the twists prepared by clues planted earlier, rather than coming out of nowhere?
- Did the story manage not to be predictable for me?
- Are the open threads resolved by the end of the book?
- Is the ending consistent with the story's premises?

## Characters
peso: 20
descrizione: Depth, development, relationships
- Does the protagonist have a personality I will remember?
- Do the characters' choices have understandable motivations?
- Do the characters behave consistently with who they are?
- Does the protagonist change credibly from the beginning?
- Do the main characters have real flaws that weigh on their choices?
- Does at least one character face an inner conflict?
- Do the secondary characters have a life of their own and are not merely functional?
- Do those who oppose the protagonist have understandable reasons?
- Do the relationships between characters change over the course of the story?
- In the dialogues, would I recognise who is speaking even without the name?

## Writing style
peso: 20
descrizione: Quality of language, readability, creativity
- Is the author's voice recognisable?
- Is the vocabulary precise, without repetition or generic words?
- Did I understand everything without having to reread for clarity?
- Is the prose free of redundancy and superfluous explanation?
- Are emotions and character shown through facts and gestures rather than stated?
- Are the images and metaphors original and not stereotyped?
- Does the tone stay consistent with the kind of story?
- Does the rhythm of the sentences fit the scene (tight in action, relaxed in reflection)?
- Is the narrative point of view handled without confusing jumps?
- Did I find at least one sentence I would like to underline or quote?

## Setting
peso: 20
descrizione: Details, atmosphere, immersion
- Are place and period clear from the start?
- Are the places rendered with concrete, sensory details, not generic ones?
- Is the setting credible, through historical research or consistency of the world's rules?
- Can I picture where the characters are in every scene?
- Does every place have an atmosphere I can perceive?
- Does information about the context arrive within the story, without descriptive blocks that stop it?
- Does the environment concretely influence events and decisions?
- Does the social and cultural context shape the way the characters are?
- Do the places reflect the themes or moods of the story?
- Would the story lose something if it were set elsewhere?

## Emotions
peso: 20
descrizione: Ability to engage, move and surprise
- Did I feel the need to keep reading?
- Did I really care what happened to the characters?
- Did I have a physical reaction while reading (laughter, being moved, tension, anger)?
- Is there at least one scene I will remember for a long time?
- Did the book make me feel different emotions?
- Were the emotions earned by the story, without melodrama or forcing?
- Did I find in the story something close to my own experience?
- Did the book make me reflect or change my point of view on something?
- Did the ending leave me with a strong emotion?
- Was I never bored by the book?
