## Content and originality
peso: 20
descrizione: Thesis, originality, completeness, relevance
- Is the book's main thesis clear?
- Is the topic treated from an original point of view?
- Does the book tackle an important subject?
- Does the book add something compared with other texts on the same subject?
- Does the author avoid banalities and clichés?
- Does the content match what the title and subtitle promise?
- Does the book stay on topic, without useless digressions?
- Does the author offer their own interpretation, not just a collection of information?
- Is the subject treated without important gaps?
- Is the content still current?

## Rigour and sources
peso: 20
descrizione: Evidence, sources, reasoning
- Does the author show a thorough knowledge of the subject?
- Are the important claims supported by evidence or sources?
- Are the cited sources reliable?
- Is it easy to trace the sources (notes, bibliography)?
- Are data and statistics used correctly?
- Does the author clearly distinguish facts from opinions?
- Are objections to the thesis taken into account?
- Are differing positions presented fairly?
- Does the reasoning proceed without logical leaps?
- Does the author avoid unproven generalisations?

## Structure and clarity
peso: 20
descrizione: Organisation, explanations, examples
- Does the order of the chapters follow a clear logic?
- Is it always clear where you are in the argument?
- Are the chapters proportioned to one another?
- Does each chapter have a clear goal?
- Are complex concepts explained in an understandable way?
- Are technical terms explained when needed?
- Are there concrete examples that help understanding?
- Does the book avoid useless repetition?
- Does the conclusion pick up the main points?
- Do the index and headings help find topics again?

## Style and quality of the text
peso: 20
descrizione: Readability, tone, editorial care
- Does the reading flow without effort?
- Is the tone suited to the subject, neither too academic nor too superficial?
- Does the author avoid a pretentious or self-celebratory tone?
- Are the sentences clear, without circumlocutions?
- Is the length of the book appropriate to the content?
- Is the text free of typos and grammar errors?
- Does the layout make reading comfortable?
- Did the book hold my interest until the end?
- Does the author make even the more technical parts interesting?
- Is the author's style personal and recognisable?

## Usefulness and impact
peso: 20
descrizione: Practical usefulness, insights, satisfaction
- Do I now understand the subject better than before?
- Did it give me ideas to apply in life or work?
- Did it make me want to explore the subject further?
- Did the main ideas stay with me?
- Would I recommend it to people interested in the subject?
- Would I go back to it as a reference text?
- Did it make me question something I took for granted?
- Was the time spent reading well spent?
- Did it give me the tools to form my own opinion on the subject?
- Overall, did the book satisfy me?
